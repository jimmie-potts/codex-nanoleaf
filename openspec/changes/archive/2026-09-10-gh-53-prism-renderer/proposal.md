- Import the approved scalable material source and reproducible component export, with archive provenance.
- Adapt assembly to the existing hub rule and two-second duration, independent connected components and permanent hexagon rim sections.

- Keep a persistent renderer with bounded local assets, validated layout replacement, passive updates and explicit disposal.
- Retain current numbering until #26 supplies conditional luminous labels and neighbour clearance. Tray artwork, services, physical frames and installation remain separate.
